# 🚦 India Road Accident Dashboard (RoadSafe India Analytics)

An interactive Excel dashboard analyzing road accident trends, severity, and risk factors across India (2018–2023).

![Dashboard Preview](dashboard_preview.png)

## 📌 Domain
Transportation & Public Safety Analytics

## 🎯 Objective
To analyze road accident data across India and uncover patterns in accident severity, casualty trends, vehicle involvement, weather impact, and geographic hotspots — helping identify actionable insights for road safety planning.

## 🧰 Tools & Skills
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query (data cleaning & transformation)
- PivotTables
- Interactive Slicers
- Conditional Formatting
- Data Visualization (pie, donut, line, and bar charts)

## 📂 Dataset Overview
- **Time period:** 2018–2023
- **Total accidents recorded:** 3,000
- **Total casualties:** 15,198
- **Fields:** vehicle type, road type, weather condition, accident location, gender, month, state, severity (fatal/serious/minor), alcohol involvement

## 🔧 Process
1. **Data Cleaning & Transformation** — Cleaned and standardized raw data using Power Query (removed duplicates, handled missing values, standardized categories).
2. **Data Modeling** — Built summary tables via PivotTables to aggregate casualties by vehicle type, road type, gender, and state.
3. **Dashboard Design** — Designed an interactive dashboard with Year and Location slicers for dynamic filtering.
4. **Visualization** — Represented trends using pie charts, donut charts, line charts, and horizontal bar charts.

## 📊 Key Insights
- **50.7%** of recorded accidents involved alcohol — a major risk factor.
- **Trucks (449)** and **buses (442)** recorded the highest casualties among vehicle types.
- Casualties were fairly evenly spread across road types, with **state highways** slightly higher (771).
- **Hazy and stormy weather** together accounted for over 40% of accidents.
- **Goa, Sikkim, and Delhi** ranked among the top states for recorded accidents.
- Accident counts show seasonal variation, peaking in **October** and dipping in **July**.

## ✅ Outcome
The dashboard enables quick identification of high-risk conditions (weather, road type, vehicle category) and locations — supporting targeted road safety interventions and policy planning.
